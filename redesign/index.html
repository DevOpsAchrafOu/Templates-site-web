FAQ

Questions before you come

Anything else? Call us at (720) 555-0142.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in during opening hours, or send us the problem first with the quote form to get a price before you come.

How much does the diagnosis cost?

Nothing. The diagnosis is free and there is no obligation to go ahead with the repair.

Will the price change after the diagnosis?

Only with your approval. If we find something different, we call you before doing anything.

How long does a repair take?

Most screen and battery repairs are done the same day, often while you wait. Water damage and laptops can take 1 to 3 days: see the price table.

Is the repair guaranteed?

Yes, every repair comes with a 90-day warranty on parts and labor.
